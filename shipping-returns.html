Shipping & returns

Last updated: 1 August 2026

Where our parcels come from

We are a small boutique, not a warehouse. Our accessories are made and dispatched from our supplier in China, which is how we keep a hand-picked catalogue at these prices. It also means delivery takes longer than a domestic order, and we would rather tell you that here than surprise you after you have paid.

How long delivery takes

The exact figure for your country and product appears on the product page as soon as you pick a destination — that number comes live from the carrier, not from a marketing estimate. As a guide:

RegionTypical delivery
United Kingdom, Spain, Italy, France, Portugal, Poland, Ireland, Japan, New Zealand4–11 days
United States, Singapore, Switzerland, Norway, Sweden, Australia5–11 days
Canada, Germany, Netherlands, Belgium7–15 days
Chile, Mexico, Brazil, Argentina, Colombia, Peru, South Korea, UAE, Nordics inland12–60 days

These are carrier working-day estimates and can stretch during peak season or customs checks. Dispatch itself takes 1–3 business days after your payment clears.

What shipping costs

Shipping is charged separately and calculated per destination — no flat rate that quietly overcharges half the world. You will see the exact amount on the product page before you add anything to the cart, and again at checkout.

Customs and duties

Most orders at our price point arrive without extra charges. If your country does apply import duty or VAT on arrival, that charge is set by your customs authority and is payable by you. We cannot calculate or absorb it.

Tracking

You will get a confirmation email as soon as your payment goes through, and a second email with tracking once the parcel is dispatched. If the tracking has not moved after 10 days, write to us — we will chase the carrier for you.

Returns

If something arrives damaged, faulty, or is not what you ordered, tell us within 30 days of delivery and send a photo to orders@thebookswanderer.com. We will replace it or refund you in full, including the shipping you paid. You do not need to send the item back for a fault — the return postage would often cost more than the product.

If you simply changed your mind, you have 14 days from delivery to tell us, and a further 14 days to send the item back. It needs to be unused and in its original packaging. Return postage is yours to pay, and we refund the product price once it arrives back with us.

Refunds go back to your original PayPal payment within 14 days of us receiving the item or accepting the fault. Nothing here affects your statutory rights.

Orders that never arrive

If your parcel has not arrived 15 days after the top of the estimated window for your country, contact us and we will either resend it or refund it in full. You choose which.

Cancelling

We can cancel an order at no cost as long as it has not been dispatched — usually a window of about 24 hours. Write to us as soon as you can and we will try.
