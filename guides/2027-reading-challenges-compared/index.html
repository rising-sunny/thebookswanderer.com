2027 reading challenges, compared

How the big ones differ, which 2027 lists are out yet (not many), and how to pick one before January turns into a month of deciding.

There is no single 2027 reading challenge. There are several, and they are different kinds of thing. One hands you 52 categories and a year. One gives you a title word each month. One is a long themed list with a bonus round. One is meant to drag you off your usual shelf. Choosing between them is mostly a question of how much structure you like, and whether you want other people involved.

Here is how the main ones work, what is published for 2027 so far, and how to choose. We are not connected to any of them. Ours is on the list too, so read that part knowing it.

How they differ

Five challenges, in plain terms. The facts come from each challenge's own pages and from Book Riot and Lit Hub, listed at the end. The “good if” lines are our opinion.

The 52 Book Club

52 categories · all year

Fifty-two prompts for the calendar year, which Lit Hub describes as vague and scavenger-hunt-like. The club votes on four new ones every September. You read at your own pace: any book finished within the year counts, audiobooks included, and the FAQ says doubling up on prompts is fine if 52 books is out of reach. There is a Goodreads group with more than 28,000 members and a StoryGraph challenge.

Good if you want a long list and a crowd to compare notes with.Less good if you want something short.

Buzzword

12 title themes + 12 cover themes · one a month

Run by booksandlala. Every month has a word or pattern the title must contain, with an optional cover challenge alongside. The 2026 list asked for “before” and “after” in January, keyboard keys in February, ordinal numbers in April, occupations in July and superlatives in December. One book a month is the whole commitment.

Good if you want a light, playful year.Less good if you like to pick by mood, since the title word narrows the shelf.

Popsugar Reading Challenge

40 standard + 10 advanced

The long themed list. The 2026 edition was garden themed and billed as a virtual book club. Book Riot notes the prompts run from broad, like “a book you meant to read in 2025,” to very specific: one asked for a character who does Pilates. There is a large Goodreads group, with more than 43,000 members.

Good if you like variety and a bonus round.Less good if oddly specific prompts will send you hunting for a book instead of reading one.

Read Harder (Book Riot)

About two dozen tasks · twelfth year in 2026

A set of tasks meant to push you out of your comfort zone and toward genres and writers you might skip. Sources give the 2026 count as 24 and as 26, so we say about two dozen. Check its rules on whether one book can count for more than one task.

Good if you want your reading stretched.Less good if you want to read only what you feel like.

Ours: the 2027 Reading Challenge

52 prompts · one a week, or 26, or 12

Fifty-two prompts, none of which tells you what to read. The whole list is free on one page, with no sign-up. You pick how many to do. There is no group, no forum and no prize: it is a list and a way to count.

Good if you want a plain list to use on your own.Less good if you want a community around it.

Pick by how you read

  • The smallest commitment: Buzzword. Twelve books.
  • Company: The 52 Book Club or Popsugar. Both have big Goodreads groups.
  • To read outside your habits: Read Harder.
  • One plain list, no group: ours.
  • Your own number: any of them. The 52 Book Club says outright that you can read at your own pace.

Whichever you pick, decide the pace first. Fifty-two books is about fifty minutes a day and twelve is about twelve. The sums are in how to read 52 books in a year.

Choose the pace before the list. Lists are easy to swap in March. Paces are not.

Running more than one

Nothing stops you running two at once, and a book can often count for both. Read the rules first. The 52 Book Club, for one, was designed around different books but says doubling up is fine if you cannot reach 52.

A pairing that makes sense: one long list for variety and one small one for fun, say The 52 Book Club for the year and Buzzword for the monthly game. Twelve extra constraints, almost no extra reading.

Questions

When does the Popsugar 2027 reading challenge come out?

We could not find a 2027 list on 7 November 2026. The 2026 list was out by December 2025, so early December is a reasonable guess. It is a guess, not an announcement.

Is there a Buzzword reading challenge for 2027?

Not announced that we could find on 7 November 2026. The 2026 list was posted in mid-December 2025, so expect the next one around then.

When is The 52 Book Club 2027 list released?

Its FAQ says the next year's challenge is usually released sometime in November, and that the exact date is always a surprise. We did not find it published on 7 November 2026.

Can I do more than one reading challenge at once?

Yes. Read each one's rules about a book counting twice. The 52 Book Club, for example, was designed around different books but says doubling up is fine if you cannot reach 52.

Which 2027 reading challenge is the easiest?

Buzzword asks for 12 books, the lightest volume, though its monthly title words narrow your choice. A looser list with a goal of 12 or 26 books is the easiest way to finish.

Do audiobooks count in reading challenges?

In The 52 Book Club, yes: its FAQ says all books are books and audiobooks are included. For the others, check their rules, or set your own.
