Gifts for book lovers they will actually use

A gift guide · updated October 2026

Buying a book for a reader is a gamble. They already have a pile waiting, they have opinions about translations and editions, and there is a fair chance they have read it. The safer gift is the thing around the book — the light they read by, the shelf it sits on, the bookmark that stops them dog-earing. Readers rarely buy those for themselves, and they use them every day.

Below are the ideas we would give ourselves, sorted by budget and then by the kind of reader you are buying for. Prices are in US dollars and include shipping.

Shopping for something specific? See the shorter guides: under $25 · for Kindle readers · Secret Santa · stocking stuffers.

Three rules before you buy

Buy for the habit, not the genre. You do not need to know what someone reads to know how they read: in bed, on a train, at a desk with a pen, on a Kindle. A gift that fits the habit always lands.
Small and good beats big and vague. A $15 metal bookmark that lives in their book for years does more than a novelty mug.
Avoid anything that harms books. Spine-cracking “book weights”, sticky notes that leave residue, cheap clips with sharp edges.

Gifts by kind of reader

The commuter

Reads standing up, one hand on a rail. A silicone page clip rides along as they turn pages, and a kraft book cover stops the paperback looking battered by Friday.

The Kindle reader

Do not buy them books. Buy the magnetic sleeve, or — if they are still on an old model — have a look at our short list of e-reader picks.

The night reader

Reads after everyone else is asleep. A bedside reading light or the stacked books lamp, which is pretty enough to leave on during the day.

The annotator and journaler

Reads with a pen. A finger book holder frees the writing hand; reading log bookmarks and the stained glass stickers go in the journal. If they log every book, the Reading Passport turns the year into a stamped travel record.

The one with the beautiful shelves

Cares how the books look as much as what is in them. A book nook, real bookends, or the lantern light. The whole decor collection is built for this person.

What not to buy a reader

Novelty socks with book puns, “I like big books” mugs, and generic bookstore gift cards when you could have chosen something. And unless you know their list, avoid this year’s most talked-about novel: odds are it is already on their nightstand.

Common questions

What do you get someone who already has every book?

Not another book. Readers are particular about what they read next, but almost nobody buys themselves the things around reading: a good light, bookends with real weight, a stand for the desk, a sleeve for the Kindle. Those get used every day.

What is a good gift for a book lover under $20?

A metal or clip bookmark, a reading log bookmark set, a silicone page clip or a wooden finger book holder. All cost between $14 and $19 and none of them depend on knowing what the person is reading.

What is a book nook?

A miniature scene — a lamp-lit street or a tiny library — built into a narrow box that slides between real books on a shelf, usually with a small LED inside. Most are sold as wooden kits that take an evening or two to assemble.
