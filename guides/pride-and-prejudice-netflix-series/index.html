Three adaptations, side by side

This is a new adaptation, separate from the two most people know. We have not seen the new one, so we do not rate it. The “good if” lines are our opinion.

The 1995 BBC series

Six episodes · adapted by Andrew Davies

Jennifer Ehle plays Elizabeth and Colin Firth plays Mr. Darcy. It has the room to follow the novel scene by scene.

Read it first, or alongside

You do not need the novel to follow the story. Reading it first lets you spot what an adaptation keeps, changes or leaves out, and that is half the fun. It has 61 chapters. Three ways to fit that in:

  • Sprint: about four chapters a day takes roughly two weeks.
  • Steady: about seven chapters a week takes nine weeks, which is how long it takes to read it alongside a six-part series.
  • After: watch first, read later. Many readers find the book funnier once they know the plot.

Where to read it free

Pride and Prejudice came out in 1813, so it is in the public domain. You can read or download it for free from Project Gutenberg or Standard Ebooks, or borrow it from your library. Annotated editions with notes on the period are not free, but they help with entails, assemblies and the militia.

A watch party in four steps

  1. Pick the night. Six parts suit one part a night, or a weekend for the whole thing.
  2. Send a short list of who is who. There are five Bennet sisters and a lot of men with similar titles, and a one-page cast list saves a lot of questions.
  3. Play along. Bingo cards of famous moments and a list of lines to listen for keep a group talking.
  4. End with a verdict. Ask each person to rank the version they watched against the novel.
