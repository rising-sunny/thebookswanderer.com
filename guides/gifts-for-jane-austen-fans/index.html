Gifts for Jane Austen fans

A gift guide · updated November 2026 · Leer en español

Jane Austen fans are easy to spot and hard to buy for: they already own the novels, often in three editions. So this list skips the books themselves and goes for things that show you know them well. Most of it is about Pride and Prejudice, because that is the one everybody quotes.

We design everything here ourselves. Prices are in US dollars, and each product page tells you whether it ships to your country and what it costs.

If you only get one thing, get the annotated mug. It has the most famous first line in English literature, highlighted and scribbled on the way a reader would. It is the gift a fan understands at first glance, and it is used every morning.

Who to give what

Someone who rereads Pride and Prejudice every year

The annotated sweatshirt. They will get every note in the margin. If you want to know what the notes are about, we wrote up what the first line really means.

A friend who has read all six novels

The Jane Austen Puzzle Book. The quiz and “Who said it?” pages are where a real fan shows off, and there is a chapter for each novel, not just the famous one.

An English teacher

The poster for the classroom wall. It is the first page they already teach, marked up the way they ask students to do it.

A colleague or a Secret Santa

The mug. It is under $25 and nobody needs to know their size.

Someone who relaxes with a pencil in hand

Color the Classics or the Commonplace Book, depending on whether they draw or write.

Someone who has never read Austen

Give them the novel and a reason to finish it. Our free 2027 reading challenge has a prompt for “a classic you pretended to have read”, and Pride and Prejudice fits it perfectly.

Questions

What is a good gift for a Jane Austen fan who has all the books?

Something that shows you know the books: the annotated Pride and Prejudice mug or sweatshirt, or The Jane Austen Puzzle Book, which has a chapter for each of the six novels.

Are the quotations accurate?

Yes. The mug and sweatshirt use Austen's text from 1813, and every quotation in the puzzle book is checked word for word against the original novels. The notes in the margins are ours.

Is the text in English?

Yes. The mug, the sweatshirt, the tote, the poster and the puzzle book use Austen's original English.

When should I order for Christmas?

The mug, sweatshirt, tote and poster by December 8 for the US and by December 1 for other countries. The printed books by December 1 wherever you are.
