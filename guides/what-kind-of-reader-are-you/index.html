How the quiz works

There are five reader types: The Cozy Nester, The Annotator, The Wanderer, The Challenger and The Classic Romantic. Each answer points to one of them, and the type with the most answers wins. If two tie, the one you chose first wins.

It is a game, not a psychological test. Real readers are a mix, and the point is a conversation starter and a free printable that suits how you like to read. You can take it as often as you like, and the result is in the page address, so you can send it to a friend.

We do not store your answers. If you ask for the free gift we keep your email, as explained on the sign-up box, and send the printable for your type.
