Common questions

What is a good Secret Santa gift for someone who loves reading?

A book-tree ornament, magnetic Christmas bookmarks or a metal bookmark: under $20, seasonal or long-lasting, and none of them require knowing what the person reads.

Is a book a good Secret Santa gift?

Only if you know their list. Readers usually have a stack waiting and firm opinions about it; an accessory is the safer choice.

Will it arrive before the office party?

Order by December 1 for most countries and by December 8 for the US. Each product page shows the delivery estimate for your country before you pay.
