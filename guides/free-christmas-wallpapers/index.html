How to set it

  • iPhone. Open the image, press and hold it and choose Add to Photos. Then, in Photos, tap Share and choose Use as Wallpaper.
  • Android. Open the image, press and hold it and choose Download image. Then open it in your gallery, choose Set as wallpaper from the menu and pick Home screen, Lock screen or both.
  • Keep your lock-screen clock in the dark top fifth: that is the part of the picture left empty on purpose.

About the picture

Theodorus van Hoytema (1863–1917) was a Dutch artist and one of the first Dutch lithographers of the Art Nouveau years. Kerstnacht is a black-and-white lithograph from 1894. The Metropolitan Museum of Art holds it and shares it in its Open Access collection, which is why we can offer it here.

The original lithograph is in the public domain. The wallpaper layout, with the sky extended for the clock, is ours.
