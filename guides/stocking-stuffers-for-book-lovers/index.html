Common questions

What are good stocking stuffers for book lovers?

Magnetic or clip bookmarks, a silicone page clip, a cat bookmark, a washi sticker book for their journal or a book-shaped tree ornament — all around $14 and all used the next time they read.

Can I put a printable in a stocking?

Yes: print the first page of the Reading Passport or a coloring page from Color the Classics, roll it with a ribbon and slip it in. The PDF arrives by email right after you pay.

When should I order stocking stuffers?

By December 1 for most countries and December 8 for the US. The printables can be bought any time, even on Christmas Eve.
