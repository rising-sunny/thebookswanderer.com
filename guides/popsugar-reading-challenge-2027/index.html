How the challenge works

The 2026 edition had a standard list of 40 prompts and an advanced list of 10 more. Book Riot notes that the prompts run from broad, such as “a book you meant to read in 2025”, to very specific: one asked for a character who does Pilates. You pick your own books and keep count; the Goodreads group is where people compare notes.

Whether 2027 keeps the same format and a theme is not known until the list is published.
