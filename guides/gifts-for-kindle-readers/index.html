Common questions

What do you buy someone who reads on a Kindle?

Not a book. A sleeve to protect the device, a reading journal or tracker so their year of reading leaves a physical record, or something for the corner where they read.

Will the sleeve fit their Kindle?

It comes in 6", 6.8" and 7" sizes and one for the 10.2" Kindle Scribe. The screen size is in the device's settings or on the box.

Why a paper reading journal for an e-reader fan?

Because e-books leave nothing on the shelf. Logging each finished book on a page — a boarding pass in the Reading Passport — gives the year something to hold.
