How many books does a 16 GB Kindle hold?

A quick guide · November 2026 · Leer en español

Short answer: a 16 GB Kindle holds several thousand ordinary ebooks. For novels and non-fiction without many pictures, that usually means somewhere between 4,000 and 10,000 books, which is more than most people read in a lifetime. Comics, picture books and audiobooks are much bigger, so the number drops fast if that is what you read.

The numbers, step by step

  1. Usable space. Not all 16 GB is free: the Kindle's own software takes part of it. Expect a little over 12 GB for your books.
  2. Size of one ebook. A typical novel downloaded from the Kindle Store takes about 1 to 3 MB, cover included. Books with many photos or maps can be 10 MB or more.
  3. Divide. 12,000 MB ÷ 3 MB ≈ 4,000 books. At 1 to 2 MB each, it is 6,000 to 12,000.

These are estimates: file sizes vary a lot between books and publishers. Your Kindle shows the real free space under Settings → Device Options → Device Info (the menu names change a little between models).

Is 16 GB enough, or should I pay for 32 GB?

For most readers 16 GB is plenty. Two reasons:

  • Your books live in your Amazon library, not only on the device. You can remove a downloaded book and download it again whenever you like, so you never lose it.
  • If you read one book a week, a few thousand books is decades of reading.

32 GB, which Amazon sells on some models, makes sense if you read a lot of comics or manga, keep audiobooks on the device, or travel for weeks without wifi and want your whole library offline.

How long would it take to read it all?

A novel is often around 80,000 to 100,000 words. At an average adult reading speed of about 250 words a minute, one novel takes 5 to 7 hours. A million words, about ten novels, takes roughly 67 hours. Filling a 16 GB Kindle with novels and reading them all would take most people several lifetimes, which is a good argument for our free 2027 reading challenge instead.
