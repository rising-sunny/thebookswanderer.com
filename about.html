About The Books Wanderer

For those who wander between pages.

The Books Wanderer is a small online boutique of reading accessories: bookmarks, page holders, Kindle sleeves, reading trackers, and the odd stained-glass sticker for a window near a good chair. The idea behind it is simple — the objects around a book should accompany the reading, not interrupt it.

We are new, and we would rather say so

This shop opened in 2026 and we are at the very beginning. We have no wall of reviews yet and no years of history to point at. What we can offer instead is straight answers: real delivery times quoted from the carrier before you buy, free shipping with the cost already in the price rather than sprung on you at checkout, and a returns policy written to be read rather than to be survived.

When customers start leaving reviews, they will appear on the site as they are written. We will not be inventing any in the meantime.

How the catalogue is chosen

We keep it deliberately small. Every item is picked by hand for one reason: it should be pleasant to live with. A bookmark that survives a bag, a page holder that works one-handed, a sleeve that does not fight the Kindle inside it. If something does not earn its place, it does not go in.

Where it ships from

Our accessories are made and dispatched by our supplier in China. That is what lets a curated catalogue exist at these prices instead of at boutique markups. The trade-off is delivery time, and we publish the real figure for your country on every product page — see shipping & returns for the full picture.

Behind the shop

The Books Wanderer is run by one person who reads too much and thinks reading deserves better objects. That is why the emails are answered within a day and why nothing here is automated to sound like a brand.

If something is wrong with your order, or you think we should stock something, write to hello@thebookswanderer.com. It reaches a person.
