The 2027 Reading Challenge

52 prompts, one a week · free printable

Fifty-two prompts for 2027, one for every week of the year. None of them tells you what to read: each one just nudges you toward a book you might not have picked up otherwise — a classic you pretended to have read, a book with a map inside, the one you've been saving.

The whole list is below. If you'd like it as a printable PDF (the 52 prompts with lines for the titles, plus a page to log the year month by month), leave your email and we'll send it straight away.

How to do it

  • Pick prompts in any order; a book can tick only one box.
  • Short on time? Aim for 12 — one a month — and keep the rest for 2028.
  • Log each finished book with the date; the PDF's second page tallies the year.
