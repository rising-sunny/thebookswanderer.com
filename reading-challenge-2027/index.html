How to use the 2027 reading challenge

  1. Choose your pace. Try 52 books for a weekly challenge, 26 for roughly one every two weeks, or 12 for one a month. Select that many prompts from the list; you can leave the others for another year.
  2. Pick your next three prompts. Start with books you already own, library finds or recommendations. Read the prompts in any order. If a book fits several, choose one box for it so each completed prompt represents a different book.
  3. Record each finish, then choose again. Write down the title and finishing date beside its prompt. Pick the next book when you are ready rather than planning all 52 in advance.

For example, start with “A book you own but never opened,” then try “Translated from another language.” When choosing “Has a map inside,” check the edition you will actually read. The prompt is a starting point; the book is your choice.

A simple way to track your reading

Use a notebook, a spreadsheet or the printable. Keep four details for each finished book: the prompt, title, author and finishing date. A short note about what stayed with you makes the list useful when you look back.

The printable has lines for titles alongside the 52 prompts, plus a second page for your monthly totals. Add each book to the month you finished it. If you keep author names or notes, record those in your own journal.

At the end of each month, count your finishes and choose a few possibilities for the next one. Adjust your goal if your time changes; there is no need to rush a long book to keep up with a weekly schedule.

Reading challenge questions

Do I have to read 52 books?

No. Fifty-two is the full list, not a requirement for taking part. A 12-book or 26-book version works too: choose the prompts you want to follow and keep one book per completed prompt.

Can I start after January?

Yes. Start with your next book and set a goal for the months you have left. You can use books already finished in 2027 if they fit a prompt; write down their original finishing dates.

Do ebooks and audiobooks count?

Yes. Choose the format that suits you: a printed book, ebook or audiobook. Library books and books you already own count too.

Do I need to sign up or buy something?

You can read all 52 prompts on this page without registering or buying anything. If you want the printable, request the free PDF by email. That also signs you up for our reading letters, at most once a month; you can unsubscribe with one click.
